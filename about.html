Our Purpose

Our Mission

To elevate the esports ecosystem through broadcast innovation and events. As a production first media organization, we focus on putting players on the world stage, hosting premier tournament events, and supporting an elite roster of global contenders. We exist to showcase the action, elevate the community, and conquer the arena.

Our Story

Ape Squad was born in 2024 from a simple, powerful idea: competitive gaming deserved a better stage. What began as a drive to host legendary community tournaments quickly revealed a deeper passion for broadcast innovation. Over the years, we have evolved from a grassroots community group into a full-scale production and media powerhouse, setting a new standard for how digital sports are played, broadcasted, and celebrated.
