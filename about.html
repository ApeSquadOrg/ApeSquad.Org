Our Mission

To forge the definitive home for competitive The Finals. We build a championship contending pro team while hosting legendary tournaments for the entire community. We provide the platform where every player can Compete, have the resources to Evolve, and the stage to Conquer.

Our Story

Ape Squad was born in 2024 from a simple, powerful idea: The Finals deserved a scene. We saw the potential for a thriving competitive ecosystem and set out to build it from the ground up: legendary tournaments where the entire community could prove their strength.
