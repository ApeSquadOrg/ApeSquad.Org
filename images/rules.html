Tournament Format

Qualifiers (Sept. 11th - NA & EU)

View participating teams at apesquad.org/Teams.

  • Format: This is a standard 3v3, 16-team, double-elimination bracket.
  • Bracket Matches: Best of 3 (Bo3)
  • The bracket will be played until only 8 teams remain (4 in the Upper Bracket, 4 in the Lower Bracket). The bracket will NOT be played to completion.
  • After the Top 8 is locked in, one final match will be played to help determine seeding for the World Finals.

World Finals (Sept. 13th)

The final championship day bracket will be posted on the website after qualifiers are complete.

  • Format: This is a standard 3v3, 16-team, double-elimination bracket.
  • Winners / Losers Bracket Matches: Best of 3 (Bo3)
  • Winners Grand Finals: Best of 5 (Bo5)
  • Bracket Reset: If required, a Best of 3 (Bo3) series will be played after a 5 minute break.

Qualifier Server Selection

Regional qualifiers must be played on their designated regional servers.

  • EU Qualifier matches must be played on EU servers.
  • NA Qualifier matches must be played on NA servers.

An exception may be made only if both competing teams mutually agree to play on an alternate server and inform a Tournament Organizer beforehand.

World Finals Server Selection

For the cross-regional World Finals, the higher-seeded team will recieve the server for the first round. Servers will alternate each subsequent round. If needed, a coin flip will determine the server of the final game.

Example (NA Team is Higher Seed):

  • Round 1: NA Server
  • Round 2: EU Server
  • Round 3 (Coin Flip)

Gameplay Rules

ALL PLAYERS MUST RECORD OR STREAM THEIR GAMEPLAY for potential review by tournament organizers. Failure to provide footage upon request may result in disqualification.

  • Class Changes: No "bait and switch" class changes. You should play the class you load into the match with.
  • Banned Items: There are currently no banned classes or specializations.
  • Substitutions: Subs are allowed before and during the tournament. You must inform a TO immediately, providing the name of the player being replaced and the substitute. Teams may play as a duo if a sub cannot be found or sub is not approved. A team will be disqualified if they are down to one player and cannot find a replacement.
  • Cheating & Exploits: Any form of cheating is strictly prohibited. This includes third-party software, scripts, and in-game exploits. Teams found cheating will be immediately disqualified.

Technical & Procedural Rules

Disconnects, Crashes, and Remakes

Player-Initiated Remake Policy

Each team is allotted one (1) technical remake per series (i.e., one total for a Bo3 or Bo5 series). This remake can be requested if a player gets disconnected, having connection issues, or their game crashes, provided the following condition is met:

  • The issue occurs before the first vault of the game has been 50% opened.

If a disconnect or crash occurs after this point, the game must continue. The affected player is encouraged to reconnect as soon as possible. Their team must continue to play with a disadvantage until the player returns.

Intentional disconnects (i.e., "rage quitting") are a serious offense and could result in an immediate forfeit of the game.

Server-Wide Issues

  • If multiple players from both teams disconnect simultaneously, the issue may be deemed a server-wide problem.
  • A TO will investigate and may order a mandatory game restart. A restart due to server issues will not count against either team's remake allowance.

No-Shows & Disqualifications

  • Teams must be checked-in at least 60 minutes before the tournament start time.
  • Teams not checked-in 30 minutes before the start time will be replaced by a reserve team.
  • We reserve the right to disqualify teams at our discretion for poor sportsmanship or rule violations.
