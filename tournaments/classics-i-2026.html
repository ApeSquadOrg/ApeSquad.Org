The Format

Survive the Weekly Cuts

APE SQUAD CLASSICS

Format: Cashout
The Objective: Accumulate points and survive the weekly "Cuts." The number of teams eliminated per playday scales based on the total number of starting teams.

THE LEAGUE

Because registration is open, the elimination math will be finalized once signups close. The goal remains the same: Narrow the field down to the Top 16 for Championship Sunday.

  • Play Days 1 & 2: Ranking and initial positioning. The first cut occurs at the end of Day 2.
  • Play Day 3: Mid-tier survival. The field is narrowed further.
  • Play Day 4: The Final Push. Only the Top 16 overall point-earners qualify for the Finals.

FLEXIBLE CUT NOTICE: The exact number of teams eliminated each night is subject to change based on registered teams. The exact "Cut Line" will be announced before each League Day.

GRAND CHAMPIONSHIP

Date: Sunday, May 10
Format: Hybrid (3v3 Bracket → Cashout Top 4 → 3v3 Grand Finals)

  • The 3v3 Bracket (Top 16): Teams seeded 1–16 based on total League Points. Double Elimination (Lose twice and you are out). Played until 4 teams remain.
  • The Cashout Finals (Top 4): Single Cashout Lobby. Best-of-3 Series. Winners side teams enter with a 3-point advantage. Highest two cumulative scores advance.
  • Grand Finals: Top 2 Teams fight it out in one final best-of-3! The winner is crowned League Champion.
