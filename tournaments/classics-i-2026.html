The Format

THE LEAGUE

Play matches in a round-robin style lobby system. Accumulate points over 4 weeks. Teams at the bottom of the leaderboard will be eliminated ("Cut") each week.

  • Days 1 & 2: Ranking and initial positioning.
  • Day 3: Mid-tier survival. The field narrows.
  • Day 4: The Final Push. Top 16 qualify for finals.

POINTS SYSTEM

Points are awarded based on placement at the end of each respective match lobby:

1ST 6 PTS
2ND 4 PTS
3RD 2 PTS
4TH 1 PT

GRAND CHAMPIONSHIP

Sunday, May 10

  • Top 16: 3v3 Bracket (Double Elimination).
  • Top 4: Single Cashout Lobby (Best-of-3).
  • Top 2: 3v3 Grand Finals (Best-of-3).
