Tournament Rules

Battle of Wits, Bans, and Chaos

EVENT FORMAT

Total Teams: 8 Teams total, split into Two Groups of (4).
Rounds: 4 Cashout rounds per group.
The Objective: The teams with the most points at the end of the day win their group!

Earning Points:

  • 1st Place: 50 points
  • 2nd Place: 30 points
  • 3rd Place: 20 points
  • 4th Place: 10 points

WILD CARDS

Contestants will battle to the end equipped with Wild Cards! Each team receives 3 wild cards to use during the event. You may use only one card per round:

  • 3x Points: Points earned this round are tripled.
  • Heist Card: Every $10,000 cashed out during this round = +10 points.
  • Ban Card: You may ban one Weapon, Gadget, and Specialization. Your team must abide by the same bans. Note: There may be multiple bans in a single round if multiple teams play this card.
