Breach Charge

The Breach Charge is a mounted gadget which, when remotely detonated, creates an explosion that breaches structures and damages opponents.

Cooldown30s
Ammo3
Damage80
Radius2.5m
View Properties & Effects

Gadget

  • Tossed: Can be thrown a short distance.
  • Single Charge: Recharges after use.
  • Retrievable: Can be picked up to restore a charge.
  • Remote Controlled: Detonate all deployed charges from anywhere.
  • Deployment Limit: Max of 3 deployed.

Charge

  • Attached: Sticks to surfaces.
  • Destructible: Can be destroyed by damage.
  • Glitchable: Disabled by Glitch effects.
  • Sound Cue: Beeps while armed.
  • Reversible: If an enemy destroys it, they get credit for the kill.

Explosion

  • Explosive: Area of effect damage.
  • Damaging: Deals damage to contestants.
  • Major Structure Damage: Destroys walls.
  • No Friendly Fire: Does not harm teammates (unless enemy triggers it).

Gateway

“A pair of deployable gateway entrances; when both are placed and activated, contestants and objects can move between the two locations”

The Gateway is a thrown gadget that creates two portals between which contestants and objects can teleport.

Cooldown40s
Ammo2
Max Range70m
Duration20s
View Properties & Effects

Gadget

  • Grenade: Thrown overhand or underhand.
  • Multiple Charges: Has 2 charges; both must be used to create the gateway.

Projectile

  • Projectile: Affected by gravity and has travel time.
  • Bounce: Bounces off surfaces.
  • Interceptible: Can be destroyed by an APS Turret.

Gateway

  • Glitchable: Disabled by Glitch effects.
  • Usable by Opponents: Enemies can also use the portals.
  • Movement: Provides a special mode of transport.
  • Not Destructible: Cannot be damaged.
