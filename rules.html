Ape Squad: Rules

Tournament Information

Note: Tournament Rules are subject to change. Please join the official Discord server and check this page regularly to stay up to date.

Organization & Links

  • Hosted by: Ape Squad
  • Servers: NA/EU Depending on Region of Tournament
  • Official Stream: ApeSquadOrg on Twitch
  • Official Discord (Required): https://discord.gg/apesquad
  • Tournament Bracket: View Bracket (Link TBA)

Prize Pool

  • 1st Place Team: 60%
  • 2nd Place Team: 30%
  • 3rd Place Team: 10%

Tournament Entry

  • To enter, please visit our tournaments page at apesquad.org/tournaments.
  • Ensure all fields are filled out correctly. Incomplete or incorrect submissions may be discarded.
  • After signing up, all team members must join the Ape Squad Discord to be assigned team roles.
  • Teams will be contacted via Discord prior to the tournament to confirm attendance.
  • The tournament is limited to 16, 32, or 64 slots per region. Teams signing up after slots are full will be placed on a reserve list.

Tournament Format

Day 1

  • Format: Standard 3v3, 16-team, double-elimination bracket.
  • Bracket Matches: Best of 3 (Bo3).
  • The bracket will be played until 16 teams remain.

Finals

  • Format: Everyone resets to winners, Standard 3v3, 16-team, double-elimination bracket.
  • Winners / Losers Bracket: Best of 3 (Bo3).
  • Winners Grand Finals: Best of 3 (Bo3).
  • Bracket Reset: If required, a Best of 3 (Bo3) series will be played.

Map and Server Selection

Map Selection Process

Map bans will be used for all matches. Captains must submit picks/bans in the #🌎map-pick-bans channel in Discord.

Map Pool

  • Las Vegas
  • Monaco
  • Bernal
  • Skyway Stadium
  • Sys$Horizon
  • Fortune Stadium
  • Nozomi/Citadel
  • Fangwai City

Server Selection

  • EU Mainstage: Played on EU servers.
  • NA Mainstage: Played on NA servers.

Score Reporting

After a full round, Captains must report scores in the #🏆score-report channel.

Please include:

  • Team Names
  • Score
  • Screenshots of results

Seeding

  • Procedure: RFI Rankings are used to seed the bracket (e.g., #1 plays #16). Random seeding will only be used for unranked teams at the bottom of the seeding bracket.

Gameplay Rules

ALL PLAYERS MUST RECORD OR STREAM THEIR GAMEPLAY. Failure to provide footage upon request may result in disqualification.

  • Banned Items: There are currently no banned classes or items.
  • Substitutions: Subs are allowed before the tournament begins. Inform a Tournament Organizer (TO) immediately.
  • Cheating & Exploits: Strictly prohibited. Teams found cheating will be disqualified. This includes third-party software, scripts, and in-game exploits.
    • See THE FINALS Fair Play Guidelines.
    • All players must adhere to THE FINALS Esports Global Rulebook, which governs player eligibility, team composition, and code of conduct.

Technical & Procedural Rules

Disconnects & Remakes

Each team is allotted one (1) technical remake per series. A remake can be requested if a player disconnects or crashes under the following condition:

  • The issue occurs before the first vault has been 50% opened.

After that point, the game must continue. Intentional disconnects are a serious offense and may result in forfeiture.

No-Shows & Check-in

  • Captains must check in via the private #✅check-in channel at least 60 minutes prior to the tournament.
  • Teams not checked in 30 minutes prior will be replaced by a reserve team.
