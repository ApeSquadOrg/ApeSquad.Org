Tournament Entry

  • To enter, please visit our tournaments page at apesquad.org/tournaments.

Tournament Format

Day 1

  • Format: Standard 3v3, double-elimination bracket.
  • Winners / Losers Bracket: Best of 3 (Bo3).
  • Played until 16 teams remain

Top 16

  • Format:Top 16 restarts back in Winners Bracket, Standard 3v3, double-elimination bracket.
  • Winners / Losers Bracket: Best of 3 (Bo3).
  • Winners Grand Finals: Best of 3 (Bo3).
  • Bracket Reset: If required, a Best of 3 (Bo3) series will be played.

  • Banned Items: There are currently no banned classes or items.
  • Substitutions: Subs are allowed before the tournament begins. Inform a Tournament Organizer (TO) immediately.

    • See THE FINALS Fair Play Guidelines.

Technical & Procedural Rules

Disconnects & Remakes

Each team is allotted one (1) technical remake per series. A remake can be requested if a player disconnects or crashes under the following condition:

  • The issue occurs before the first vault has been 50% opened.

After that point, the game must continue. Intentional disconnects are a serious offense and may result in forfeiture.

No-Shows & Check-in

  • Captains must check in via the private #✅check-in channel at least 60 minutes prior to the tournament.
  • Teams not checked in 30 minutes prior will be replaced by a reserve team.

Content Creation & Media Policy

We want to see your clips! Ape Squad maintains an open media policy to encourage players, streamers, and community members to share the action. You are encouraged to watch-party, record, and edit footage from the tournament, provided you adhere to the guidelines below.

Permitted Usage

  • Transformative Content: You are free to create and publish highlights, montages, news recaps, and short-form clips (TikTok/Shorts) using footage from the Official Ape Squad Broadcast.
  • Stream POV: Players are fully permitted to stream, record, and upload their own Point of View (POV) without restriction.
  • Monetization: You are allowed to monetize any content you create based on the tournament (e.g., YouTube ad revenue, Twitch bits), provided it follows the restrictions below.

Restrictions & Attribution

  • No Full Broadcast Uploads: You may not re-upload the official broadcast in its entirety or upload unedited full-match VODs to YouTube or other platforms. Content must be edited or transformative (e.g., highlights or commentary).
  • Credit Required: When using official broadcast footage, you must credit Ape Squad in the description, tag, or caption.
  • No Misrepresentation: You may not present content in a way that suggests you own the event or represent the Ape Squad brand officially.

Point Break Rules

Please read the specific Attack/Defend rules carefully.

Match Flow & Scoring

  • Bracket: 16 Teams, Single Elimination.
  • Series Structure: Two games per match. Teams will take turns Attacking and Defending in Point Break.
  • Timer: Attackers have exactly 15 minutes to destroy objectives.
  • Hard Stop: After 15 minutes, the match ends immediately, regardless of remaining lives or objective health.
  • Scoring: 1 Point is awarded for each objective destroyed.
  • Map Selection: Each Team picks one map to veto and the games are played on the remaining map.
The Breaking Point (Tie-Breaker)

If both sides earn the same score after two games, the Breaking Point is reached.

  • Format: Each team selects ONE Champion to represent their squad.
  • Mode: 1v1 Team Deathmatch (Single Round).
  • Loadout: Any class and any gear is allowed.
  • Stakes: The winner of the 1v1 moves their team to the next stage. The loser is eliminated.

Restrictions & Fair Play

During a Point Break match, "anything goes" with one major exception regarding objective interaction.

  • Forbidden Exploits: Do not use exploits to prevent the objective from being armed or defused.
  • Specific Examples:
    • Moving objectives entirely out of the Attacker zone.
    • Utilizing equipment (like Barricades/Goo) in a glitched manner that removes the "Interact" prompt.
  • Penalty: Violating these rules will result in punishment at the admin team's discretion (Point deduction or Disqualification).

Technical

  • Check-In: Captains must check in 60 minutes prior to bracket start.
  • Disconnects: If a player disconnects, the game continues. No restarts unless the server itself crashes.
  • Recording: At least one member per team must record or stream their gameplay for dispute resolution.
